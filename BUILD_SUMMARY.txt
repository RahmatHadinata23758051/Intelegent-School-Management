RISK SCORING ALGORITHM:
========================

Academic Score (60% weight):
- Grade 85+: 10 points
- Grade 75-84: 25 points
- Grade 65-74: 50 points
- Grade 55-64: 70 points
- Grade <55: 100 points

Behavioral Score (40% weight):
- Minor: +5 points
- Moderate: +15 points
- Major: +30 points
- Severe: +50 points
- Multiple violations cumulative

Final Risk Level:
- Low: 0-20 points
- Medium: 21-50 points
- High: 51-100 points

AUTOMATIC UPDATES:
- Grade created/updated/deleted → Risk recalculates
- Violation created/updated/deleted → Risk recalculates
- All calculations atomic and consistent
